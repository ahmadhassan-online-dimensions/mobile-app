Lens guide

Find your reading strength in two minutes.

A simple estimate you can do at home. For the most accurate result, have your eyes tested by an optometrist.

1. Your age

Close-up vision usually changes gradually from the early 40s, so age gives a typical starting point.

2. Quick reading test

Take off any reading glasses. Hold your screen at your usual reading distance, about 35–40 cm. Tap the smallest line you can read comfortably.

Screens differ in size, so treat this as an estimate, not an eye test.

Understanding lens strength

What do the numbers mean?

+1.00 is the lightest magnification. Higher numbers magnify more.

Are both lenses the same?

Yes, ready reading lenses have the same strength in both lenses. If your eyes differ, choose prescription lenses.
